YOUR PHOTOS. YOUR CHOICES.

Privacy, explained.隐私说明

Photo editing is local. Here is what happens to your photos and face details.

English简体中文

Effective / 生效日期: 2026-09-26

Operator / 运营主体: Zhenqing Hou

English

01

Photo processing happens on your iPhone

BetterTake processes your selected or captured photos on your iPhone. The app does not upload your photos or face data to a processing server. It has no account system, advertising SDK, or analytics tracker.

02

Face details are used for editing

The app temporarily holds photos and detected face landmarks in memory to compare expressions, match the same person across your selected sequence, and combine photos. It does not create or store face identity templates, and it does not use these details to identify you outside the editing session.

03

You control what is kept

Returning to the home screen or successfully replacing the current photo set clears the previous editing session. Canceling a replacement keeps the current session. The app does not maintain a separate photo history. Images saved to Photos remain in your library until you manage or delete them there. Imported originals and full-resolution exports may use app-private temporary files. Copies are removed when no longer referenced; abandoned session files are cleaned when the app next initializes temporary storage. Work already being processed may finish before temporary memory is released.

04

Permissions are your choice

Camera access is used to capture a short burst. You can use the system photo picker without camera access or granting access to your entire photo library. When you save, the app requests permission to add photos. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

05

iCloud and sharing follow your choices

A photo stored in iCloud may need to download through Apple's photo picker before import. Your Photos and iCloud settings determine whether saved images sync between devices. Sharing sends the result through the destination you select in the system share sheet; the receiving service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

06

This website and support

This website has no photo upload form, sign-in, advertising pixels, or visitor analytics added by BetterTake. The hosting platform processes ordinary web requests needed to deliver and secure the site, which may include an IP address and browser headers. This is separate from photo editing in the app. If you email support, the contact details and information you send are used to respond to your request. You can explain an issue without attaching personal photos.

07

Policy changes

If the app's data handling changes, this page and its effective date will be updated to describe the new behavior.

Contact and privacy requests

简体中文

01

照片在你的 iPhone 上处理

BetterTake 在你的 iPhone 上处理选择或拍摄的照片，不将照片或人脸数据上传到处理服务器。应用没有账号系统、广告 SDK 或分析追踪工具。

02

人脸信息只用于编辑

应用会在内存中临时保留照片和检测到的人脸关键点，用于比较表情、匹配同组照片中的人物，以及合成照片。应用不会创建或保存人脸身份模板，也不会用这些信息在本次编辑以外识别你的身份。

03

由你决定保留什么

返回首页或成功换入另一组照片后，应用会清除上一组编辑内容；取消重选会保留当前内容。应用不另建照片历史记录。已保存的照片留在系统相册，你可以在那里管理或删除。导入原图和全分辨率导出可能使用 App 私有临时文件。临时副本在不再被引用时删除；异常退出遗留文件会在下次初始化临时存储时清理。正在执行的图像处理可能需要结束后才释放临时内存。

04

权限由你选择

相机权限用于连续拍摄一组照片。不允许相机访问，也可以使用系统选图器；选图不需要授予整个相册的访问权限。保存时，应用会请求向相册添加照片的权限。你可以在 iPhone 的系统设置中修改权限。

05

iCloud 与分享由你控制

保存在 iCloud 的照片可能需要通过 Apple 的系统选图器先下载，再导入应用。保存的照片是否在设备间同步，由你的相册和 iCloud 设置决定。分享时，结果会通过你在系统分享面板中选择的目标发送，接收服务按其自身条款和隐私政策处理。

06

本网站与使用帮助

本网站不提供照片上传或登录功能，也未添加 BetterTake 的广告像素或访客分析工具。托管平台会处理提供和保护网站所需的普通网络请求，其中可能包含 IP 地址和浏览器请求头；这与应用内的照片编辑是独立的。若你发送客服邮件，所提供的联系方式和内容将用于回复请求。你可以描述问题，无需附上私人照片。

07

说明更新

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页及生效日期，说明新的处理方式。
