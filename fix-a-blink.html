BETTERTAKE PHOTO GUIDE · IPHONE

Fix a blink in an iPhone group photo using another shot.

Someone blinked in a group photo you want to keep? If another shot has the expression you want, BetterTake lets you choose it. Import 2–8 photos with similar angles and people in roughly the same positions. Editing runs on your iPhone, without an account.

By Zhenqing Hou, BetterTake’s developer · September 26, 2026 · For version 1.0.1

Start with two usable photos

You need another photo in which the same person has the expression you want. BetterTake does not generate new eyes, smiles or faces from a single photo. Version 1.0.1 accepts different photo sizes and capture times; a steady, short sequence still works best.

About this example: the source photos depict AI-generated fictional adults. The editing screenshot shows BetterTake’s real analysis and composition workflow on an iPhone. This illustrates the workflow, not a promise that every face or photo will work. Interface labels may vary by version.

1. Import the original photos

Open BetterTake and choose 2–8 photos of the same people, or take a short burst in the app. Import your original photos, not a screenshot of this guide or an App Store advertisement. Photos stored in iCloud may need to download first.

2. Tap the person and choose an expression

Tap the person you want to change, then select an available expression from another photo. Here, the middle person’s open-eyed expression comes from Photo B. Eyes and mouth change together from that source photo; they are not independently generated.

3. Compare, zoom in, and save

Compare with the original and zoom in to inspect the eyes, mouth and surrounding area. Undo if the change does not look right. Saving creates a new image without overwriting the original. Export is a JPEG at the original pixel dimensions, not a promise of lossless encoding.

What if no expression is available?

A visible face is not enough: the source expression must also match and align reliably. A large angle change, movement, covered faces or low light can limit the choices. Try a more similar photo, keep the original expression, or choose a whole source photo. Successful import does not guarantee an available expression for every person.

With only one photo, BetterTake cannot invent the missing expression. Single-person mode helps you choose a whole photo rather than combine expressions.

Do my photos leave my iPhone?

Analysis and editing happen on your iPhone without uploading photos to a processing server. iCloud may download originals through Apple’s services, and sharing uses the destination you choose.
