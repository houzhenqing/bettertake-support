## Get started

1. Take a short burst in BetterTake, or import 2–8 photos of the same people. Use a similar camera angle and keep people in roughly the same positions. Version 1.0.1 accepts different photo sizes and capture times.
2. Tap a person and choose an available expression from another photo. Eyes and mouth change together from that real source photo.
3. Zoom in, compare with the original, and undo changes if needed. Save to Photos when you are happy with the result.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can BetterTake fix a blink from just one photo?

Expression editing needs another usable photo of the same person from the same moment. BetterTake does not generate a new face, eyes, or smile. Single-person mode lets you choose a whole source photo.

### Why are some expressions unavailable?

Movement, a different camera angle, covered faces, low light, or unreliable alignment can reduce the available choices. Use a steady, short sequence. If either eyes or mouth cannot be replaced completely, BetterTake keeps that person's original expression and explains why. You can still choose a whole photo.

### Are my photos uploaded?

Analysis and composition run on your iPhone. BetterTake has no account, remote photo-processing service, advertising or analytics SDK. Photos stored in iCloud may need to download through Apple's photo services before import. Sharing happens only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet.

### What happens to photo quality?

Saving and sharing export a JPEG at the original pixel dimensions. This is not a promise to preserve RAW/TIFF encoding, bit depth, or lossless quality. Large images take longer and need available storage. Import supports up to 200 megapixels and 32,768 pixels on either edge, up to 1 GiB encoded per image and 8 GiB per set. From version 1.0.1, photos in a set may have different dimensions and capture times. Importing successfully does not guarantee that every expression can be matched and aligned.

### Why can't I save or use the camera?

Allow Camera access for in-app capture and permission to add photos when saving. Import uses the system photo picker. If you previously denied access, check BetterTake in iOS Settings. For a save failure, check available storage, retry, and verify the result in Photos before leaving the edit.

### Are my originals changed? Can I resume an unsaved edit?

BetterTake does not overwrite your original Photos items. Saving creates a new image. It does not provide a saved-project library or account-based restore; save the result before leaving a session. App-private temporary files are used during import and export. Saved and shared copies are managed in Photos or the destination you chose.

## Older versions

Version 1.0.0 requires matching photo dimensions and capture times within one minute. Update to 1.0.1 or later for more flexible imports. Similar angles still matter for usable expressions.
