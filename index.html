FROM A FEW PHOTOS TO ONE YOU LIKE

Choose a person. Find their moment.

01

Start with a short sequence

Take a burst in BetterTake, or import 2–8 still photos taken from the same angle. Keep the camera and people in roughly the same positions.

02

Choose an expression

Tap a person and explore their available moments. Zoom in, compare with the original, and undo a change whenever you need to.

03

Keep the photo you like

Save to Photos, then share if you choose. If an expression cannot be used, keep the original or pick a whole photo instead.

YOUR PHOTOS, ON YOUR IPHONE

Real expressions. Local processing.

BetterTake uses the photos you choose to compare expressions and combine them on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics trackers in the app.

A steady camera helps.

Use photos from the same short sequence. Big movements, covered faces, or low light can limit the choices. Eyes and mouth change together from your selected photo. If they cannot both be applied, the original expression is kept.
