English

Which photos should I choose?+

Choose 2–8 still photos of the same people from similar angles. Version 1.0.1 accepts different photo sizes and capture times. Keep the camera steady and people in roughly the same positions. Successful import does not guarantee a usable expression for every person. You can also capture a short burst inside BetterTake.

How do I change an expression?+

In group review, tap a person in the photo or the person selector, then choose an available expression below. Zoom in to see details, compare with the original, or undo the last change. Select the original candidate to restore that person's original expression. Save to Photos when you are happy with the result.

Why are there fewer expression choices than photos?+

A person must be matched reliably and their face must be clear enough to use. Large head turns, movement, face coverings, or low light can remove a candidate. BetterTake keeps the original when it has no suitable alternative. You can still view the whole photos and choose one to save.

What changes when I choose an expression?+

The eyes and mouth are taken together from the same selected photo. If either cannot be applied, BetterTake keeps that person’s complete original expression. Changes to other people remain. You can choose another available expression or a whole source photo.

What happens with a single person?+

Single-person review lets you choose a whole source photo rather than combining face regions. Use View all photos to see every imported or captured frame.

A photo will not import. What can I do?+

If a photo is stored in iCloud, download it to your iPhone in Photos first. Then select it again. Different photo sizes are supported in 1.0.1, but heavy cropping can make face matching harder. Each photo can be up to 200 megapixels, 32768 pixels on its longest side and 1 GiB encoded; each set can contain up to 8 GiB of encoded files. Editing uses smaller previews; saving and sharing output JPEG at the original pixel dimensions. RAW support and lossless or original TIFF bit depth are not promised. Try fewer or smaller photos if the set is too large. Damaged files, animations, and Live Photo frame selection are not supported.

Will choosing another set lose my current result?+

Your current review stays available until the new set finishes loading, analysis, and composition. Canceling that replacement or an import validation failure brings you back to the current result. Returning home clears the editing session; save any result you want to keep first.

Why can I not save or share?+

Wait for the photo to finish updating and exit original comparison. Saving needs permission to add photos: open iPhone Settings and allow photo access for BetterTake. If a write fails, your result is kept so you can retry. Sharing appears after the current result has been saved. Your photo is sent only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet.

Can I use BetterTake without camera access?+

Yes. Choose photos from your library with the system picker. If you want to capture a burst later, allow camera access in Settings. If the camera is temporarily unavailable, retry it or use existing photos.

Do I need an account? Are photos uploaded?+

No account is needed. The app processes photos and face landmarks on your iPhone; it does not upload them to a processing server. Downloading from iCloud and using the system share sheet follow your Apple and destination-service settings. See the privacy policy for details.

简体中文

应该选什么样的照片？+

请选择角度相近、人物相同的 2–8 张静态照片。1.0.1 支持不同尺寸和拍摄时间的照片；相机和人物尽量保持位置。导入成功不代表每个人都有可用表情。也可以直接在 BetterTake 中连拍。

怎样给一个人换表情？+

在合照编辑页，点照片中的人或人物选择栏，再点下面可用的表情。可以放大看细节、查看原图或撤销上一步；点原图候选即可恢复这个人的原样。选好后保存到相册。

为什么表情候选比导入的照片少？+

人物需要可靠匹配，人脸也需要足够清楚。转头幅度大、移动、遮挡或光线不足，都可能让某张照片无法成为候选。没有合适替代时会保留原样；你仍可以查看全部照片并选一张保存。

选择表情后，会替换哪些地方？+

眼睛和嘴巴从同一张所选照片一起替换。如果任一区域无法应用，会完整保留这个人的原表情，不影响其他人的修改。你可以选择其他可用表情，或改选整张照片。

单人照片怎么用？+

单人模式选择整张来源照片，不进行人脸区域合成。点“查看全部照片”即可浏览本组所有导入或拍摄的照片。

照片导入失败，该怎么办？+

如果照片在 iCloud 中，请先在系统相册中下载到手机，再重新选择。1.0.1 支持不同尺寸的照片，但大幅裁剪可能增加人物匹配的难度。单张最多 2 亿像素、最长边 32768 像素、编码文件 1 GiB；整组编码文件最多 8 GiB。编辑使用较小预览，保存和分享按原始像素尺寸输出 JPEG，不承诺 RAW 支持、无损编码或保留 TIFF 原位深。若照片过大，请减少张数或选较小的照片。暂不支持损坏文件、动画或从实况照片中挑选不同帧。

重新选一组，会丢掉当前结果吗？+

新一组照片完成加载、分析和合成之前，会保留当前编辑内容。取消重选或导入校验失败，会回到当前结果。返回首页会清除编辑内容，请先保存想保留的照片。

为什么无法保存或分享？+

请先等待照片更新完成，并退出原图比较。保存需要添加照片权限，可在 iPhone 设置中为 BetterTake 开启相册权限。保存失败会保留结果，可以重试。当前结果保存成功后才会出现分享入口；只有你在系统分享面板选择目标后，照片才会发送。

不开放相机权限也能用吗？+

可以。使用系统选图器从相册选择照片即可。如果之后需要连拍，再到系统设置中允许相机访问。相机暂时不可用时，也可以重试或选择已有照片。

需要账号吗？照片会上传吗？+

无需账号。照片和人脸关键点在你的 iPhone 上处理，不上传到处理服务器。iCloud 下载和系统分享遵循你在 Apple 及接收服务中的设置，详见隐私说明。
