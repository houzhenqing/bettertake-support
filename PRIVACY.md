## English

BetterTake is developed by Zhenqing Hou. This policy describes how BetterTake handles photos and other information.

### We do not collect app data

BetterTake does not require registration or sign-in. It has no advertising, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, developer-operated cloud sync, or remote photo-processing service. We do not receive, sell, or share your photos, face-analysis results, or app usage data through the app.

### Photos and face processing on your iPhone

Photos you capture or select are processed on your device. BetterTake uses Apple Vision and local image processing to detect faces and facial landmarks, match a person across the selected sequence, evaluate available expressions, and compose the chosen result. This processing is limited to the editing session; it is not used to identify you by name or build a server-side biometric profile. Photos, landmarks and matching results are not sent to us or a remote model provider.

Working images and analysis are held for the current session. Imported originals and full-resolution exports may use app-private temporary files. Temporary copies are removed when no longer referenced; abandoned session files are cleaned when the app next initializes its temporary storage. BetterTake does not provide an account-based backup or persistent editing-project library.

### Permissions and Apple services

Camera permission is requested for in-app photography. Import uses Apple's system photo picker to access the items you select. Permission to add to Photos is requested when saving. BetterTake does not require microphone or location access. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Photos stored in iCloud may be downloaded through Apple's services before import, and images saved to Photos may sync according to your Apple settings. These system services are controlled by Apple and your settings, not a BetterTake photo server.

### Saving, sharing and deletion

Saving adds a new JPEG to Photos without overwriting the original item. BetterTake shares a result only when you choose to use the system share sheet and select a destination. The receiving app or service handles that copy under its own privacy practices. Delete saved images in Photos and shared copies at their destinations; deleting BetterTake does not remove those copies. Removing the app removes its app-private local data under iOS app-deletion behavior.

### Support and this page

If you email support, we receive the message and attachments you choose to send and use them to answer your request. Please avoid sending private photos or other people's personal information. This voluntary email correspondence is separate from the app's on-device processing. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence by contacting the same email address.

This public support page is hosted by GitHub. Visiting it or using your email provider is subject to those services' privacy practices. BetterTake does not add website analytics or tracking scripts to these documents.

### Children and changes

BetterTake does not collect personal data through the app from anyone, including children. If data handling changes, we will update this policy and obtain any required permission before introducing the new processing. The effective date appears above.

## 简体中文

BetterTake 由 Zhenqing Hou 开发。本政策说明照片及其他信息的处理方式。

### 我们不收集 App 数据

BetterTake 无需注册或登录，不包含广告、分析 SDK、跨 App 跟踪、开发者运营的云同步或远程照片处理服务。我们不会通过 App 接收、出售或分享你的照片、人脸分析结果或使用数据。

### iPhone 上的照片与人脸处理

你拍摄或选择的照片在设备本地处理。BetterTake 使用 Apple Vision 和本地图像处理来检测人脸及面部特征点、匹配所选连续照片中的人物、判断可用表情，并合成所选结果。处理仅服务于当前编辑流程，不用于识别你的姓名，也不建立服务器端生物识别档案。照片、特征点和匹配结果不会发给我们或远程模型提供方。

工作图和分析结果用于当前流程。导入原图和全分辨率导出可能使用 App 私有临时文件。临时副本在不再被引用时删除；异常退出遗留的文件会在 App 下次初始化临时存储时清理。BetterTake 不提供账号备份或持久化编辑项目库。

### 权限与 Apple 服务

App 内拍摄需要相机权限；导入通过 Apple 系统照片选择器访问你选中的项目；保存时申请添加照片权限。BetterTake 不需要麦克风或定位权限。你可在 iOS 设置中管理授权。

iCloud 中的照片可能在导入前通过 Apple 服务下载，保存到相册的图片也可能按你的 Apple 设置同步。这些系统服务由 Apple 和你的设置管理，不属于 BetterTake 的照片服务器。

### 保存、分享与删除

保存会向相册添加新 JPEG，不覆盖原项目。只有你主动使用系统分享面板并选择目标时，BetterTake 才分享结果；接收方按自己的隐私规则处理副本。已保存的图片请在相册删除，已分享的副本请在相应目标中管理；删除 BetterTake 不会删除这些副本。移除 App 时，iOS 会按 App 删除机制移除其私有本地数据。

### 支持邮件与本页面

如果你发送支持邮件，我们会收到你主动发送的文字和附件，并用于回复请求。请尽量不要发送私人照片或他人的个人信息。自愿邮件沟通与 App 内的本地处理相互独立。你可以通过同一邮箱要求删除支持邮件记录。

本公开页面托管于 GitHub。访问页面或使用邮箱服务时，适用相应服务的隐私规则。BetterTake 未在这些文档中添加网站分析或跟踪脚本。

### 儿童与政策更新

BetterTake 不通过 App 收集任何人的个人数据，包括儿童的数据。如果处理方式变化，我们会更新本政策，并在启用新的处理方式前取得必要授权。生效日期显示在页首。
